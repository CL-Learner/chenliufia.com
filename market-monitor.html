Chen's Take

The headline is growth. The question is quality.

APE is useful, but incomplete

APE ranking should be read together with single premium reliance, channel mix, and product class. The same headline APE can represent very different economics.

Participating business remains the centre of gravity

A market led by savings and whole life products makes bonus governance, illustration discipline, and policyholder expectation management central issues.

Mainland visitor data needs a footnote

Separate 2025 Mainland visitor statistics are not available while IA reviews non-local policyholder data collection. That absence is itself a market signal.
